How it works

One to one. Your own space.

Every topic gets its own space. Veya teaches in live voice you can interrupt and answer back, like a patient friend who happens to know the subject.

Underneath, it keeps a memory of what you understand and where you stall. So it decides what's next, and makes the first move.
